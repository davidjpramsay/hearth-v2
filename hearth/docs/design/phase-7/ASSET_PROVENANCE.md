# Phase 7 asset provenance

All Phase 7 concept and demo-photo assets are original Hearth materials made
with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-08-05. The people are
fictional Ezra and Maya demo characters. No Apple, Skylight or other product
branding, artwork, screenshots or real household photos were used.

## Concept

- `concepts/photos-tv.png` — high-fidelity 16:9 television Photos concept,
  generated with the current rendered Hearth Today screen as a shell/style
  reference only.

The concept prompt required the existing Hearth navigation, warm ivory canvas,
eucalyptus/sky/ochre palette, large featured image, mixed photo grid, 28-pixel
television copy, a visible focused thumbnail and `Start ambient`; it prohibited
media controls, credentials, filesystem paths, Apple/Skylight branding and
text over detailed photos.

## Product demo photos

- `public/demo/family-breakfast.webp` — existing fictional breakfast photo
- `public/demo/photos/coastal-picnic.webp`
- `public/demo/photos/park-football.webp`
- `public/demo/photos/bush-camping.webp`
- `public/demo/photos/garden-morning.webp` — portrait-orientation test asset
- `public/demo/photos/thumbs/*.webp` — locally compressed thumbnail derivatives

The photo prompt set used the existing fictional breakfast image only to keep
Ezra's curly hair/blue shirt and Maya's long brown hair/ochre shirt consistent.
Separate prompts placed them at a Perth coastal picnic, playing park football,
at a supervised bush campsite and watering a garden. Every prompt required
natural family-safe photography, useful crop margins, no brands, no text and
no watermark. Final PNG outputs were inspected and converted locally to
stripped WebP display and thumbnail assets before use.
