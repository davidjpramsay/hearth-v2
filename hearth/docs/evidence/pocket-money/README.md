# Pocket-money rendered evidence

Captured with installed Playwright Chromium because no interactive browser/IAB controller was available in the toolset.

The retained screenshots cover the proportional weekly Chores summary at 3840×2160, 1920×1080, 1366×768, 390×844 and 844×390, plus the parent Pocket money administration page at both iPhone orientations. The demo completes one of Ezra's three chores, producing 33% and A$4.00 of the A$12.00 weekly amount.

Visual inspection checks:

- weekly percentage and amount remain secondary to the child's name and daily chore actions;
- three-child television columns remain within one viewport, with unconfigured children called out clearly;
- the phone layout uses one readable column with a required dollar amount, payday and payment action;
- warm Hearth surfaces, eucalyptus progress and blue D-pad focus remain consistent in light and dark themes;
- there is no star balance, reward catalogue or per-chore point value in the active screens.
